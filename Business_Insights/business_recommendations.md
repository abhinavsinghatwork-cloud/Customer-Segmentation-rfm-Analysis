# Business Insights & Recommendations

## Key Findings

1. **At Risk is the largest segment:** 118 customers (54.63%) are At Risk.
2. **Champions lead on average RFM metrics:** average frequency 7.23, average monetary 509.25, and average recency 158.49 days.
3. **Lost customers are least engaged:** average frequency 1.00, average monetary 19.26, and average recency 735.08 days.
4. **Loyal customers show repeat-purchase behaviour:** average frequency 6.30 and average monetary 230.93.

## Recommended Actions

- At Risk: test targeted retention messaging and relevant offers; track reactivation rate and incremental revenue.
- Champions: offer loyalty benefits and personalised engagement to protect high-value relationships.
- Loyal: encourage repeat purchases with relevant cross-sell and upsell recommendations.
- Lost: test cost-conscious win-back campaigns and stop spending on campaigns that do not produce incremental value.

## Measurement Plan

Track campaign reach, conversion/reactivation rate, repeat purchase rate, incremental revenue, redemption rate, and campaign cost by segment.

*All findings describe the current dataset/dashboard snapshot. Recommendations are hypotheses to test, not proven campaign results.*
